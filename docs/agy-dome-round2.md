# Dome Review: Round 2 (v6 Draft)

**Image Viewed:** `/tmp/ayasofya-v6-dome-draft.png`

**Judgement:**
- **Curvature Improvement:** Yes, the curvature and overall volume are materially improved. The 18-plate rise successfully captures the majestic, rounded silhouette of the real dome, completely resolving the squashed appearance of the original render. 

**Critical Visual Issues:**
- **"Pinecone" Stepping:** Because the steeper 18-plate height field is covered exclusively with shallow 2-plate-high slopes (`11477`) and 1x1s, the pieces cannot cleanly bridge the steep vertical jumps near the base of the dome. This results in a heavy "staircase" or pinecone-like texture with exposed vertical gaps.
- **Recommendation for Final Render:** To fix this rough stepping, you should use steeper pieces (like 45° 1x2 slopes `3040` or stepped standard bricks) at the steep base of the dome, reserving the `11477` curved slopes for the middle tiers where the mathematical slope matches their physical geometry.
