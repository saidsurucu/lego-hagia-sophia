# Dome Review: Round 3 (v6 Refined)

**Image Viewed:** `/tmp/ayasofya-v6-dome-refined.png`

**Comparison vs. v6 Draft:**
- The introduction of 45-degree `3040b` slopes on the outer radial band (beyond 6.5 studs) is highly successful. It cleanly eliminates the deep vertical gaps and "pinecone" texture that plagued the base of the previous draft.
- The physical gradient transition—from the steep 45-degree base up into the shallower `11477` curved slopes—beautifully matches the underlying 18-plate spherical cap mathematics.

**Final Visual Judgement:**
- **Curvature & Silhouette:** Excellent. The scale and volume now correctly reflect the majestic proportions of the real Hagia Sophia dome, without becoming a bulbous onion.
- **Stepping vs. Geometry Failure:** The draft version suffered from a clear geometry failure where shallow parts could not bridge the steep vertical drops. In this refined version, that failure is resolved. Any minor stepping that remains along the curves is simply the inherent, accepted nature of approximating spheres on a discrete LEGO Cartesian grid. It looks structurally cohesive, aesthetically authentic, and ready for final rendering.
