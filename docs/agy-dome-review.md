**Radial Formula:** 
A true spherical cap profile, rising steeply at the base and shallowing smoothly toward the crown.

**Most Important Visual Changes:**
- **Variable Slopes:** Transition from steeper base pieces to flatter crown pieces to create a realistic dome silhouette, rather than repeating a single uniform slope.
- **Finer Patching:** Use 1x2 and 1x1 pieces instead of large 2x2 blocks along the curves to reduce grid-like faceting and better hug the circular radius.
